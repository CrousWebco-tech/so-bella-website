# Admin Panel Guide for So Bella Hair & Beauty Lounge

## 🎯 How to Access the Admin Panel

1. Go to your website: `yoursite.com/admin`
2. Enter your email address and submit
3. Check your email and click the sign-in link (magic link)
4. You're in! 🎉

## 📋 What You Can Edit

### Business Info Tab
- Business name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Physical address
- **Business hours** for each day

### Hero Section Tab
- Main tagline (the short inspiring text)
- Main headline (large welcome text)
- Button text (Book Now, View Gallery, etc.)

### Social Links Tab
- Instagram link
- Facebook link
- TikTok link

### Services Tab
- Contact your developer for advanced edits

---

## 💾 How to Save Changes

1. Edit any information in the form
2. Click the **"💾 Save Changes"** button
3. You'll see a "✅ Saved successfully!" message
4. Your changes are stored!

---

## 🔐 Sign-in Notes

This admin uses passwordless email sign-in (magic links). If you prefer a password-based login or additional admin accounts, contact your developer and they can enable Supabase Auth settings or create user accounts for your team.

---

## ❌ If You Get Locked Out

- Check the password (it's case-sensitive)
- Try again after 30 seconds
- Contact your developer if you can't remember it

---

## ✅ What You'll See After Saving

Changes are saved to your website database (Supabase) so updates sync across devices. If you don't see changes immediately, try refreshing the page or contact your developer.

---

## 📞 Support

Questions? Contact your developer with:
- What you're trying to edit
- What error you see (if any)
- Your browser name and version

---

**Remember:** You never need to touch any code or GitHub! Just use the forms in `/admin` to update everything.

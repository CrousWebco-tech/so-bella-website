# So Bella — Your Website Guide

Hi! This is everything you need to run your website from your phone. No tech skills needed. Take it slow — you only set most of this up once.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. On your phone, open your web browser (Safari or Chrome).
2. Go to **your-website-address.com/admin** (your developer will give you the exact link).
3. Type your **email** and **password** (your developer sets these up for you the first time).
4. Tap **Log In**.

Tip: Save the /admin page to your home screen so it's one tap away.
- iPhone: tap the Share button → "Add to Home Screen".
- Android: tap the menu (⋮) → "Add to Home screen".

---

## 2. The dashboard — what each tab does

After logging in you'll see tabs across the top. Swipe sideways to see them all.

**🏢 Business Info** — Your name, email, phone, address, opening hours, and your WhatsApp number.

**✨ Hero Section** — The big welcome text at the top of your website.

**💇 Services** — (managed with your developer for now).

**🖼️ Gallery** — Add and remove your photos.

**🔗 Social Links** — Your Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok links.

After changing anything, scroll down and tap **Save**. You'll see a "Saved" message.

---

## 3. Adding photos to your gallery (the part you'll use most)

1. Tap the **🖼️ Gallery** tab.
2. Pick a **Category** (e.g. Hair Extensions, Lashes).
3. Add a short **Title** if you like (optional).
4. Tap **Choose photo** and select a picture from your phone.
5. Wait for "Added to your gallery!" — that's it. It's live on your website straight away.

**To remove a photo:** scroll to "Your photos", tap the **×** in the corner of any photo, and confirm.

Photo tips: bright, clear photos look best. Square or upright photos fit nicely.

---

## 4. Changing your WhatsApp "Book Now" number

Every "Book Now" button on your site opens WhatsApp to chat with you.

1. Go to **🏢 Business Info**.
2. Find **WhatsApp Number**.
3. Type your number with your country code, no spaces (UK example: `447503130010`).
4. Tap **Save**.

---

## 5. Updating your opening hours

1. Go to **🏢 Business Info**.
2. Edit each day's hours (e.g. `9:00 AM - 5:00 PM`, or type `Closed`).
3. Tap **Save**.

---

## 6. Common questions

**I forgot my password.** Ask your developer to reset it — it's quick.

**My change didn't show up.** Make sure you tapped **Save**, then refresh the website page.

**Can I break something?** No. You can only edit text and photos. If anything looks odd, just message your developer.

**Who can log in?** Only your email address. Nobody else can get into your dashboard.

---

That's everything. Welcome to your new website! 💖

## Hair Extension Aftercare ✅

### General Aftercare
- Plait your hair extensions at night to prevent tangles.
- Don't wash your extensions for 48 hours after installation, and wash them a maximum of twice a week thereafter.
- Only use the recommended brush whilst brushing extensions to prevent pulling on the roots.
- Avoid chlorine and sea water coming into direct contact with the extensions. Wear a high bun or swimming cap if necessary.
- Schedule a maintenance/refit appointment within 6–8 weeks of installation to prevent matting.
- Avoid sunbathing for too long as it can dry out the extensions; apply heat protection spray every hour.
- Apply Argan oil to the ends daily to keep them soft and shiny — use on wet or dry hair.

### Washing, Drying & Styling
- Wet the hair using warm water and apply the recommended hair extension shampoo.
- Gently lather the shampoo into the roots and ends using a soft zigzag motion to prevent pulling on the roots. Rinse and repeat once more.
- Wash the hair backwards under running water. Do not tilt your head forwards over the edge of a bath, as this can damage the roots.
- Wring out the extensions and apply a generous amount of conditioner or mask to the mid-lengths and ends.
- Comb through with a wide-tooth detangling comb and leave to soak for 2–5 minutes. Avoid conditioner on the roots.
- Thoroughly rinse with warm water. Wring out and wrap in a towel for 5–10 minutes (extensions hold more water than natural hair).
- Apply Argan oil to the mid-lengths and ends of the damp hair and comb through with a wide-tooth comb, working upwards from the ends in sections.
- Dry the hair in sections with a hot-air brush tool.
- Finish the look with straighteners or curlers.

## Lash Aftercare ✅
- Avoid getting lashes wet for 24–48 hours.
- Avoid oil-based products and mascara.
- Avoid sunbeds for 24 hours.
- Do not pick or pull your lashes; brush them daily with a lash wand.
- Do not use an eyelash curler.
- Use lash shampoo daily to avoid build-up of bacteria and debris.
